Super Benji Adventures tells the story of Benji, a superhero in the League of Grace, as he fights against Dr. Banana and the evil forces of the Banana Republic to recover the Banana Gem and save the world. The webcomic is a comic adaptation of the story of the Super Benji Show and Super Benji Movie, with the storyline adapted to the webcomic format and rewritten for continuity.

The Super Benji series was originally created in 2017 by Themadpunter and triangle5820. Since then, the series has recieved multiple games (most originally created on Scratch), as well as the series' biggest project, the Super Benji Show and the Super Benji Movies. Super Benji Adventures is a remake, continuation, and upgrade of the story originally told in these movies.

For more information about the Super Benji series in general, you can visit the offical Super Benji website, home to the Super Benji Show and Super Benji Movie series at superbenji.us.
