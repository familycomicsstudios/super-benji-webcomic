Inside a space station high above the Earth, we find BENJI, a superhero in the League of Grace, and DR. BANANA, a criminal mastermind and leader of the BANANA REPUBLIC. Sitting in a pedestal in the center of the room is the BANANA GEM, a powerful artifact that can grant supernatural abilities to those who possess it. The Banana Gem was stolen from the League of Grace by Dr. Banana, and Benji has been sent to Dr. Banana's space station to retrieve it. A thrilling story is about to unfold...
