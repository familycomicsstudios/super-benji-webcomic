The Super Benji Webcomic tells the story of Benji, a superhero in the League of Grace, as he fights against Dr. Banana and the evil forces of the Banana Republic to recover the Banana Gem and save the world. The webcomic is a comic adaptation of the story of the Super Benji Show and Super Benji Movie, with the storyline adapted to the webcomic format and rewritten for continuity. The webcomic is being created by Themadpunter, who draws and develops the whole webcomic.
